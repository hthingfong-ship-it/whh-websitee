About WHH

Offline strength, presented with greater clarity online.

Established in 1995, WHH Group has built its business around food import and export, processing, wholesale and retail services. Today, the company serves diverse customer segments through food service, retail and wholesale channels.

An established supply partner since 1995

WHH’s foundation is built on long-standing customer relationships, disciplined execution and a well-established service system that supports both day-to-day operations and business growth.

What makes WHH relevant to buyers

  • One-stop food solutions for retail, wholesale and food service markets.
  • Experience across sourcing, processing, storage, replenishment and delivery.
  • Operational capability designed to support business customers, not only one-off purchases.
  • Core values centered on integrity, quality and long-term partnership.
